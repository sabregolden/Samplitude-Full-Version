## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Audio Recording Tools** — Capture high-quality audio with advanced recording options.
- **MIDI Mixing and Editing** — Effortlessly mix and edit MIDI files alongside audio tracks.
- **Built-in Effects Section** — Utilize native effects, including bass and guitar emulators, to enhance your sound.
- **ASIO and MME Compatibility** — Ensure low-latency performance with broad driver support.
- **VST Instrument & Effects Insertion** — Expand your creativity with various VST plugins for unique sound manipulation.

### Advanced Features:
- **BeatBox** — A virtual beat box with its own sound library for dynamic beat creation.
- **Revolta Synthesizer** — Dive into analog sound design with this versatile synthesizer.
- **Vintage Effects Suite** — A collection of guitar pedals designed to process any audio signal effectively.
- **Vita Sampler** — A user-friendly sampler designed for quick and intuitive sound sampling.
